ACT & SAT Prep is an independent study app for the ACT and SAT college entrance exams. It is not affiliated with ACT, Inc. or the College Board; this is stated in Settings, in the description, and on the support site. All questions, passages, and tutorials are original.

No account or login is required.

To see the subscription: open the Settings tab and tap "Upgrade to Pro", or tap any locked tutorial, flashcard deck, or mock exam.

Free without purchase: the diagnostic, a daily question, 10 practice questions per subject, 2 tutorials per subject, the English / Reading & Writing flashcard decks, and Mock Exam 1.
